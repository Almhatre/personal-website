<Margin tag="§ 00 · opening">
**Throughline.** The only honest summary I have of my working life: I enjoy
asking *what does matter do when you push it to a limit?* Black holes, fusion plasmas,
eighty-eight keys, a barbell: all different limits, still the same question.
</Margin>

<Pen>
"Physics is the only thing worth studying" — a note to self at age 17. I have no physics degree, nor degree at all today.
</Pen>

<Body>

<Lede>
Every project listed further down this page is a restatement of the same
curiosity, re-asked in a different medium. I did not realize this until
writing, but it is *obvious* to me in retrospect.
</Lede>

As a teenager I wanted to know how black holes could exist when we don't 
understand anything past the event horizon. A few years later,
how could we possibly recreate the inner workings of the sun in a terrestrial
fusion reactor? Right now I am running a company that sits a little to the side of those
questions, which is part of why I wanted to write this page: to keep the
thread visible.

The point of this page is not to list accomplishments. It is to make the
thread visible to me, and — if you've bothered to read it — to you.<Footnote n="1" />

</Body>
