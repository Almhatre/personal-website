I have played piano since I was five. My teacher then is my teacher now. She has watched me go
from Mary Had a Little Lamb to the Chopin Ballades. She knows and cares to
know little about physics. She is one of the most consistent presences in my
life who is not family. Without her, I would be dramatically less artistic, introspective, philophsical
and a lot more boring.

Whether it is piano, blackholes, nuclear fusion or a startup, you are often doing the same thing:
you push a system against a boundary. You measure what comes back. You adjust. You repeat until the
response function is what you want.

A pendulum is the simplest example, so here's one. Drag the bob; the motion
is harmonic at small angles, messier at large ones. Your fingering on a scale
is harmonic when you're relaxed and messier when you tense up. The same
differential equation, with different constants.
