<Body>

In the spring of my junior year I left Stanford, went through YC, and started
a company called **Photonium**. We build AI tools for optical design. Optical
engineers spend most of their day in Zemax or Code V nudging surface
parameters, running merit functions, tolerancing existing designs, and
waiting. A good chunk of that work is pattern-matching against an internal
library of tricks. That is the part we automate.

The reason I care: neutral-atom quantum computing, space instruments,
lithography, precision measurement, and a lot more cool physics is bottlenecked by optics. If optical design gets ten times cheaper,
those fields get easier. That is the thread back to the rest of this notebook.
